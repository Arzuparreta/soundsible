## Fixes

- During the fade, an unrequested regression of more than 50 ms disables
  phase corrections **for that transition** and returns the incoming song to
  1× only once. The file and the fade are kept, without seek or reload.
  Beat alignment is sacrificed when the executor cannot sustain it;
  the following transitions keep their normal beatmatch.
- The return ramp is also abandoned if the clock goes back >50 ms or
  advances less than half a second per second in a 1–2 s window.
  The normal speed band is close to 1×: that deficit is an execution
  anomaly, not a musical tempo difference. Pauses, seeks and timer
  gaps longer than 2 s restart the deficit observation.
  `mix.rate_fallback` leaves evidence with `clock_regressed` or `clock_starved`.
- Before scheduling curves or taking ownership, the mixer requires real
  progress of the incoming song, `readyState >= 3`, without pause or seek, also in manual
  skips. While it loads, the outgoing song keeps playing. The natural end
  keeps the existing path of ordinary handover of the prepared track.
- The unlock uses 20 ms of valid silent PCM instead of an empty WAV.
  Unlock by gesture and the protection of already loaded tracks are kept.

The thresholds are clock continuity tolerances, not calibrations of
musical taste. The fix is a conservative recovery on evidence of
failure; it is not equivalent to fixing or certifying the iPhone renderer.
Android does not run `HTMLMediaElement.playbackRate` or this unlock WAV;
its contract and its plans do not change.

## Validation

- Audio service tests: clock regression without seek, stable fallback
  until the fade completes without reloading, ramp with insufficient progress,
  healthy gradual ramp and manual skip without samples. The tests
  of phase, silent seeks, natural end, cancellation and graph mixing are kept.
- The previous immediate skip test expected ownership before observing
  progress: that obsolete expectation is updated, keeping the check
  that the prepared incoming song is not reloaded.
- New browser test: the PCM is decoded as 20 ms of silence and
  `HTMLAudioElement.play()` resolves from a real gesture.
- Before/after control: the new expectations also run
  against the previous code in an isolated worktree: four expected failures
  (regression, stuck ramp and premature ownership in the two manual paths),
  versus 110/110 audio tests with the fixes.
- Complete interface/audio suite: 1,804/1,804; Python traces: 26/26.
  The operator report includes `mix.rate_fallback` even outside
  handoff windows. Mobile/desktop Chromium: 357 passed and 71 skipped.
  Mobile/desktop WebKit: 348 passed and 80 skipped (Docker, one worker,
  read-only mount). Remote checks in PR #405.
- CI of PR #405: emulator shard 3/8 failed in
  `ProgramDjRecoveryTest.httpSilentArmedRefinement` (expected position 0,
  read 22666 µs). The test read `positionUs()` in the same block as
  `player.pause()`, and the `AudioTrack` keeps reporting the frames already
  delivered to the HAL for a moment. The test now waits for the
  position to stop advancing before capturing it. No Android code changes.
- Afterwards `ProgramDjRecoveryTest.httpStarvedOutgoing` failed with
  `Prepared cue changed by late refinement`, the same as in `v0.29.1` and in the
  branch of PR #403. #403 made `applyRefinement` accept a prepared and
  silent mix if at least 5 s remain before its cue; the test attempted
  the refinement as soon as the incoming song had PCM, and the result depended on whether
  that happened before or after that margin. With a mix, the test now
  attempts it when the mix is already playing and checks that the window does not change.
- `DjNetworkTest.httpIncomingBodyStall` and the Windows x64 smoke test
  failed once each and passed or were rerun without changes: there is no
  evidence that they are related to this PR.
