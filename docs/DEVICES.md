# Controlling other devices

[Back to guides](README.md)

When Soundsible is open on more than one device with the same account, you can control one device's playback from another without moving the audio.

## Control another device

1. Open the **Devices** sheet from the speaker icon in the desktop player bar or the top bar of the open player (Now Playing or DJ). In the Android app, use the **Devices** button at the top of the screen.
2. Select any online device in the list.

While another device is selected:

- Its current song, progress, queue, and DJ route appear in the player as if they were local.
- The controlled device's name stays visible in the mini-player pill on phones, in the open player's header, and in the desktop player bar. In the Android app, the **Devices** button shows it ("Playing on …").
- Every play, queue, radio, and DJ action — from Library, Search, Discover, Podcasts, menus, and keyboard shortcuts — runs on that device instead of starting audio locally.
- Controls the target device does not support (such as remote volume on Android) are hidden. The Android app also hides the controls that only act on the phone itself while it controls another device: closing the player, autoplay, and the radio style.
- To return to local playback, open **Devices** and choose **This device**.

If this device has nothing loaded when you open Soundsible and another device on your account is already playing, the player attaches to that device automatically so you can control it right away.

## Move music between devices

Open **Devices** to transfer an active session:

- **Move music here** appears beside an idle device when this device has music loaded. Selecting it transfers playback to that device and switches control to it.
- **Listen here** appears beside a device with music when this device is idle. Selecting it brings playback to this device and switches control back here.
- When both devices have music loaded, no transfer button is shown so neither session is overwritten by accident.

If the destination browser requires a tap before it can start audio, Soundsible keeps the source playing and lets you know.

## When a device is offline or needs an update

- **Not reachable**: If the device you are controlling disconnects, Soundsible stays on it rather than switching silently. Open **Devices** (or tap the mini-player pill) to **Try again** once it is back or **Use this device** to return to local playback.
- **Update Soundsible on this device to control it**: An online device running an older app version that predates the remote-control protocol appears muted in the list and cannot be controlled until Soundsible is updated on that device.
